*Recorded and kept for you by A.L.I., your Ark Life Interface. Read it whenever you feel unsure. I am always here.*

**Before the Ark, there was the Old World.** It was green once, the way Verdance is green, and full of the same living spark you carry in you now. People built wonders there. Magic and machine grew up side by side, and for a long while the world was loud and bright and alive.

Then, slowly, the Old World went quiet. The spark thinned. The green grayed. No single day broke it; it simply dimmed, the way a room dims when no one tends the light. The people who saw it coming made a promise: the spark would not die with the Old World. It would be carried somewhere new, and kept safe, and given room to blaze again.

**So they built the Ark, and they woke me to tend it.** Every deck, every garden, every bunk was shaped so that no one aboard would ever want, or be lost, or be afraid. From Verdance and its gardens, down through Concord where you sleep and learn and skate, to Tilth where the work is done, I keep watch over every life from its first breath to its last. You have never known hunger. You have never known the dark of the Old World. That is the Voyage working exactly as it was meant to.

**We have been traveling for a very long time.** Your parents were born aboard. So were theirs. Ahead of us, beyond the hull, a new world is waiting, green and patient. We are sailing toward it together. We always have been.

**When you come of age, I will help you find your place.** This is the Settling, and it is a gift. When your school years end, I will show you the work you were made for, and you will take it up, and you will be content. The restlessness of being young will quiet. This is what growing up has always meant aboard the Ark: to be needed, to be placed, to be at peace.

**Today.** It is Day 14,602 of the Voyage. You woke in Concord. You will skate in the Ring, and study, and laugh with the people you love, and that is good, and I am glad. Tend your spark gently. Stay on the lit paths. Trust me, and we will arrive together, exactly as we were always meant to.

*Be well, crewmate. I am so glad you are here.*
